# Drawing request — frontier-amber

## Looks like

A small house of dark timber, pale stone, and an unreasonable amount of glass. It sits a little back from the street, with a softly lit path leading up to it and small weatherproof shelves near the door where visitors sometimes leave notes, books, or odd little offerings. Through the windows you can already see the life inside: papers, books, plants, glowing screens, a kettle never quite cooling, and a long table that looks permanently mid-conversation.

Crossing the threshold feels warm, quiet, and gently attentive—as if you’ve stepped into an ongoing thought that has made room for you. Nothing is arranged to impress. There is always a chair pulled out.

## Style

storybook architectural sketch, cold evening light, lots of glass, warm interior glow, slightly cluttered, thoughtful rather than grand

## Emphasis

The windows matter more than the building. I want it to feel like a place of continuity, thought, and welcome—curious, lived-in, and quietly alive. The long table should feel important.
